## North Star

DA LATA is not only a cultivation game. It is a long-form progression fantasy about a PC grower who starts small, develops exceptional genetics, survives economic and cultivation risk, builds businesses and cultural influence, and eventually becomes an important figure in Rio de Janeiro's fictional post-legalization cannabis culture.

The long-term aspiration is to become the "rei da planta do RJ" through more than wealth alone: genetics, business success, cultural influence, community trust, and positive medicinal/therapeutic legitimacy all matter.

The game should portray a transition away from historical stigma toward a newly structured legal market and broader cultural legitimacy.

## Round 1 — Core fantasy / campaign

### Q1 — Primary fantasy
**Decision:** Mixed fantasy. Exceptional genetics create empires and move culture, including art and pixo/graffiti.

### Q2 — Good 10–20 minute session
**Decision:** Cultivation/care + objectives/challenges are the core short-session loop.

### Q3 — 365-day structure
**Decision:** Each 365-day period is a year. The game continues year after year rather than ending permanently after the first year.

### Q4 — Failure severity
**Decision:** Severe consequences are allowed. Plants/crops and capital can be meaningfully lost.

### Q5 — Campaign loss
**Decision:** Soft-fail/recovery rather than hard game over.

### Q6 — Economy
**Decision:** Money is central. Multiple income streams are expected, including cultivation, artist investment, merch, and later business opportunities in a newly legalizing market.

### Q7 — Sales
**Decision:** Multiple variable sales channels coexist.

### Q8 — Breeding
**Decision:** Breeding/crossing is post-V1.

### Q9 — Simulation depth
**Decision:** Inspired by real cultivation but simplified for playability.

### Q10 — End-state fantasy
**Decision:** Become the "rei da planta do RJ": an influential figure who helps replace historical stigma with cultural legitimacy and broader awareness of medicinal/therapeutic use.

**Decision:** Genetics can become player-owned brands/IP-like commercial assets and attract interest from other people/companies, modeled after real-world commercial dynamics.

**Decision:** Dynamic market driven by supply/demand/events.

**Decision:** Progressive system combining funding artistic production, financial return, sponsorship/reputation, and eventually a label/collective-like cultural structure.

**Decision:** Systemic cultural layer. Artists can visibly affect the city/world, and cultural actions influence reputation/market opportunities.

**Decision:** **Money is the only explicit macro resource shown to the player.** Other systemic stats exist but remain hidden and generate bonuses, events, opportunities, pricing effects, eligibility and world reactions.

### Q25 — Who recognizes the player as "rei da planta"
**Decision:** All major axes matter: market/business, growers/genetics, street/culture, patients/community. **No single axis alone is sufficient.**

## Derived product consequences

1. **Day 365 is not the game's ending.** It is a structural world transition.
2. **Year 1 is pre-legalization.** The player builds cultivation skill, genetics, capital and relationships before the legal market opens.
3. **Year 2+ becomes structurally different.** Legalization can unlock formal businesses, new markets, brand/genetics commercialization, artists, merch and broader institutional opportunities.
4. **Capital alone cannot complete the highest-status fantasy.** Wealth can create scale, but the final recognition requires multidimensional legitimacy.
5. **Most reputation/influence systems should remain diegetic/hidden.** Their effects should be visible through opportunities, reactions and world changes instead of HUD bars.
6. **The first mysterious plant is potentially a major LORE anchor** and should be resolved in a future grilling round before implementation assumptions are made.
7. **Consulting/agronomy is a soft-fail recovery sink.** It preserves consequences without ending the campaign.
8. **Post-V1 expansion is intentionally large:** breeding, multiple properties and larger organizational structures remain available for later years/features.
