# Growing Rio — GDD v0.1

## High concept
Management / narrative simulation set in a fictionalized Brazilian cannabis economy. The player grows a small operation into a larger enterprise while balancing production quality, cash, reputation, regulatory exposure (`Heat`) and institutional influence.

The game deliberately abstracts cultivation and illicit-market activity. It is designed as strategy, not as real-world instruction.

## Player fantasy
Start with one improvised grow space. Learn the economy, establish buyer relationships, professionalize operations, navigate a fictional regulatory landscape and eventually unlock the legendary `DA LATA` endgame storyline inspired by the cultural memory of the Brazilian "Verão da Lata".

## Core loop
1. Maintain the current grow batch.
2. Advance time and absorb random operational events.
3. Harvest when the batch matures.
4. Choose a sales channel with different reward/risk profiles.
5. Reinvest cash into capacity, compliance, research and reputation.
6. Participate in fictional institutional systems to change future constraints.

## Strategic currencies
- **Cash** — operating capital.
- **Heat** — aggregate regulatory / enforcement pressure.
- **Reputation** — trust and market prestige.
- **Influence** — abstract access to institutional progression.
- **Research** — later unlock for genetics and process upgrades.
- **Community** — later unlock for social legitimacy and cooperative routes.

## Campaign structure
### Act I — O Quarto
One grow space, one batch at a time, basic buyers, 30-day cycles.

### Act II — O Negócio
Employees, multiple rooms, equipment, contracts and operating expenses.

### Act III — Dois Mercados
Formal and parallel-market channels become strategically distinct. Parallel activity remains an abstract contract system rather than a logistics simulator.

### Act IV — O Sistema
Fictional regulators, associations, public hearings and policy proposals alter economic rules.

### Act V — DA LATA
A mythic research chain culminates in the fictional `DA LATA` cultivar and a special finale inspired by the cultural story of the "Verão da Lata".

## Vertical slice v0.1
Playable target:
- 30-day cycle.
- One room.
- Abstract health / progress model.
- Care action once per day.
- Harvest and quality grade.
- Licensed retailer sale.
- Parallel-market sale with higher return and higher Heat.
- Institutional participation action generating Influence.
- Random events.
- Fail state through time or severe negative cash.

## Design boundaries
- No real cultivation recipes, dosages, nutrient schedules, climate targets or yield-optimization instructions.
- No real trafficking routes, concealment, evasion, sourcing or distribution procedures.
- No real politicians, parties or targeted political persuasion.
- Politics is represented through fictional institutions and systemic modifiers.
