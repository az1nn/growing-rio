# 3JS-005 Institutional visual reference brief

Status: **repository-derived / no external runtime assets**

## Why this scene
The canonical product map orders the major surfaces as Operação → Mercado → Cidade → Institucional → Arquivo/Pesquisa. The first three already have delivered Three.js presentations. Institucional remains a Control-only surface and is therefore the next bounded visual gap.

## Visual source of truth
This wave does not need a new external moodboard. It derives from the accepted in-repository 3JS-002 Grow Room style lock and the delivered Market/City continuity passes:
- fixed orthographic miniature/cutaway presentation;
- concrete / warm plaster / dark metal / teal / warm wood / terracotta family;
- chunky low-poly procedural silhouettes;
- cool structural illumination with restrained warm practicals;
- portrait-first quiet UI reserve;
- zero authored texture baseline and shadowless Web/mobile rendering.

## Institutional translation
The scene is an **original fictional administrative/civic forum**, not a representation of any real government or political system:
- public threshold and low waiting bench;
- generic participation/service desk;
- three equal proposal pedestals with identical visual treatment;
- archive/storage rhythm and abstract process rails;
- balanced practical lights that do not privilege a proposal;
- no flags, seals, ballot boxes, chambers, party colors, maps, national symbols, slogans or real institutional architecture.

## Neutrality rule
The three proposal forms are presentation placeholders only. They have equal geometry, material, height and lighting. Three.js contains no policy names, effects, prerequisites, recommendations, ranking or selected winner.
